## Overview

Vang Vieng is one of the most affordable places in the world to fly in a **hot-air balloon**—drifting quietly above vertical limestone cliffs, emerald rice paddies, and the winding **Nam Song River** as morning fog lifts off the valley.

## Highlights

* **Sunrise Hot-Air Balloon (5:45 AM pickup):** ~35–40 minute flight over the karsts. Sunrise flights offer calmer winds and dramatic valley mist; sunset flights glow golden over the western cliffs.
* **Nam Song Kayaking:** Half-day guided paddles start north of town near **Tham Nam (Water Cave)** and glide past riverside villages and limestone peaks back into central Vang Vieng.
* **Riverside Sunset:** Grab a table at a wooden bungalow bar overlooking the Nam Song as balloons drift past the mountains at dusk.

## Booking Tips

* Book balloons 1 day ahead in high season (Nov–Feb) through your guesthouse or reputable local operators (e.g., Above Laos).
* Bring a light jacket for early-morning winter flights (Dec–Jan mornings can dip to 12–15°C).
