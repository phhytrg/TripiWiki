## Overview

Standing at the corner of Lane Xang Avenue and Setthathirath Road, **Wat Si Saket** (built in 1818) is the oldest temple still standing in its original form in Vientiane. Right across the street sits **Haw Phra Kaew**, the former royal temple that once housed the Emerald Buddha.

## Highlights

* **Cloister Wall Niches (Wat Si Saket):** Walk the shaded perimeter gallery holding more than 6,800 silver, ceramic, and wooden Buddha images.
* **Carved Wooden Doors & Library:** Look for the Burmese-influenced wooden roof tiers and the quiet courtyard frangipani trees.
* **Haw Phra Kaew Museum:** Admire the tall gilded columns, carved doors, and outdoor gallery of bronze standing Buddhas ("Calling for Rain" pose).

## Practical Tips

* **Dress code:** Shoulders and knees covered (sarongs are available to rent at the gate).
* **Walkable loop:** Both temples are a 5-minute walk from the Presidential Palace and 10 minutes from the Mekong promenade.
