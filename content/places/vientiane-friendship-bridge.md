## Overview

Opened in 1994, the **First Thai–Laos Friendship Bridge** spans 1,170 meters across the Mekong River, connecting **Vientiane (Hadxayfong)** to **Nong Khai (Thailand)**. Uniquely, its center deck carries the **metre-gauge railway track** used by cross-border passenger trains between Vientiane and Bangkok.

## Highlights

* **Mekong River Viewpoint & Sunset:** Watch boats and cross-border shuttle buses traverse the wide Mekong bend—just 10 minutes before **Buddha Park (Xieng Khuan)**.
* **Thanaleng Border & Duty-Free Area:** Browse the border plaza and see where the historic **Thanaleng Railway Station** (Laos's very first international train station, now complemented by Khamsavath Station) sits just north of the bridge.
* **Cross-Border Gateway:** If continuing overland to Thailand, shuttle buses cross the bridge every 15–20 minutes after Lao exit immigration.
