## Overview

**Pha That Luang** (Great Stupa) is the national symbol of Laos. Its 45-meter gold-leaf spire sits on a quiet esplanade northeast of central Vientiane.

## What to Do

* **Morning walk at Pha That Luang:** Arrive before 9:00 AM to beat the heat and see locals offering alms and incense.
* **Patuxai Victory Monument:** A 15-minute tuk-tuk or bicycle ride away; climb to the top for panoramic views down Lane Xang Avenue.
* **Mekong Riverside Night Market:** Head to Chao Anouvong Park at sunset for street food, grilled river fish, and Beerlao overlooking Thailand.

## Getting Around

* **LCR High-Speed Train:** Vientiane Station is ~30 minutes outside the center; take the green shuttle bus or LOCA taxi app.
* **Getting North:** Trains depart daily to Vang Vieng (~1h) and Luang Prabang (~2h).
