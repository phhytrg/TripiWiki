## Overview

Known as the "Cat City", **Kuching** sits along the winding Sarawak River and is one of Southeast Asia's most charming, walkable cities—blending Malay *kampung* villages, historic Chinese shophouses, indigenous Dayak crafts, and world-class street food.

## Highlights

* **Kuching Waterfront & Darul Hana Bridge:** Stroll the river promenade at sunset, cross the S-shaped pedestrian bridge, and ride a traditional *sampan* wooden boat across the river for MYR 1–2.
* **Bako National Park:** 45 minutes + a 20-minute boat ride from Kuching; hike headland trails past sea stacks, pitcher plants, bearded pigs, and wild **proboscis monkeys**.
* **Semenggoh Wildlife Centre:** Just 30 minutes south of Kuching—spot semi-wild orangutans emerging from the forest canopy during morning/afternoon feeding times.
* **Must-Try Food:** **Sarawak Laksa**, **Kolo Mee**, and **Kek Lapis** (colorful layered cakes).

## Connecting North to Miri, Brunei & Sabah

* Kuching is ~800 km southwest of Miri. Most travelers take a 1-hour domestic flight **Kuching (KCH) → Miri (MYY)** or **Kota Kinabalu (BKI)**, or drive the upgraded **Pan Borneo Highway** north across Sarawak.
