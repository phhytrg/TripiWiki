## Overview

Located right along **Thadeua Road (Km 11)** in Hadxayfong District—directly on the way to Buddha Park—the **Lao Disabled Women's Development Centre (LDWDC)** empowers women with disabilities from across Laos through vocational training in traditional handloom weaving, sewing, and handmade mulberry (*saa*) paper crafts.

## What to Do

* **Guided Centre Tour:** Meet the artisans, see traditional wooden floor looms in action, and learn how natural dyes and *saa* paper are made.
* **Hands-On Craft Workshop:** Try weaving or paper-making yourself (book 1 day ahead for half-day workshops).
* **Ethical Handicraft Shop & Lao Lunch:** Buy fair-trade silk scarves, bags, and notebooks directly from the makers, and enjoy a freshly cooked traditional Lao meal on-site.

## Getting There

* **On the way to Buddha Park:** ~20 minutes southeast of central Vientiane on Thadeua Road (Public **Bus #14** stops right along Thadeua Road; or ask your LOCA/tuk-tuk driver to stop before continuing to Buddha Park).
