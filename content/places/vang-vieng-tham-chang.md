## Overview

Just 1.5 km south of town, **Tham Chang (Tham Jang)** is the easiest cave to reach in Vang Vieng—accessed across a bright orange suspension bridge over the Nam Song and up 147 concrete steps. Historically used as a 19th-century refuge, its cool limestone gallery opens onto a natural balcony overlooking the river valley.

## Combine with Pha Ngern (Silver Cliff)

* **Pha Ngern Viewpoint:** About 4 km west of town, this steeper jungle trail climbs to **three distinct viewpoints** (Viewpoint 1: ~35 min, Viewpoint 2 & 3: ~60–75 min).
* **Why hike Pha Ngern:** It's higher and far less crowded than Nam Xay, with dramatic 360-degree views of Vang Vieng's jagged karst ridges.

## Practical Tips

* **Cold spring at Tham Chang:** Right at the base of the cave stairs is a crystal-clear spring pool—great for a quick dip after walking from town.
* **Pha Ngern gear:** Bring at least 1.5L of water, grippy hiking shoes, and small Lao Kip notes for the village trail entrance fee.
