## Overview

Roughly 3.5 hours north of Luang Prabang, **Nong Khiaw** sits in a dramatic gorge carved by the Nam Ou River. It's the gateway to northern Laos trekking and boat-only villages.

## Highlights

* **Pha Daeng (Red Cliff) Viewpoint:** 1.5-hour steep hike starting at dawn. You'll stand above a sea of morning fog filling the river valley.
* **Boat to Muang Ngoi Neua:** Take the 1-hour wooden longtail boat upriver to a car-free village, or hike further to Ban Na and Tad Mok waterfall.
* **100 Waterfalls Trek:** A full-day guided jungle trek scrambling up cascading limestone falls.
