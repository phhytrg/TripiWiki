## Overview

Rising at the center of grand **Lane Xang Avenue**, **Patuxai** ("Victory Gate") blends Parisian triumphal-arch proportions with intricate Lao mythological motifs—*kinnari* (half-bird, half-woman figures), lotus reliefs, and five ornate stupa towers. Ten minutes away on foot sits **That Dam (Black Stupa)**, a quiet 16th-century brick stupa said to be guarded by a seven-headed *Naga* dragon.

## Highlights

* **Climb to the 7th-Floor Rooftop:** Take the spiral stairs past souvenir floors to the top terrace for 360-degree views down Lane Xang Avenue toward the Presidential Palace and Pha That Luang.
* **Patuxai Park & Musical Fountains:** Come around 5:30–7:00 PM when the arch is illuminated and locals gather around the fountain plaza.
* **That Dam & French Quarter Cafes:** Stroll around the weathered brick stupa, then stop at nearby bakeries and Lao coffee shops along Rue Chanthakoumane.

## Practical Tips

* **Easy walking loop:** Combine Patuxai, That Dam, and **Wat Si Saket** into a relaxed 2–3 hour afternoon walk.
