## Overview

While Blue Lagoon 1 gets packed with tour buses, **Blue Lagoon 3** sits 17 km deep in Vang Vieng's western karst valley—surrounded by sheer limestone walls, bamboo huts, and emerald rice fields. Half the fun is the scenic ride out there through quiet rural Lao villages.

## Highlights

* **Swimming, Rope Swings & Bamboo Rafts:** Wide, deep turquoise spring water with free bamboo rafts to pole across the lagoon and a wooden zipline over the pool.
* **Explore Nearby Cave:** A short 5-minute walk from the lagoon leads to a small, quiet limestone cave tucked into the cliffside.
* **The Western Karst Loop (~35 km):** Ride a scooter or dune buggy past **Nam Xay Viewpoint**, **Blue Lagoon 1**, and continue west to **Blue Lagoon 3** and **Blue Lagoon 2** for a full-day countryside loop.

## Road & Safety Tips

* **Dusty/bumpy final 8 km:** Expect unpaved gravel and potholes—ride slowly on a scooter (semi-automatic or sturdy 125cc recommended), and bring sunglasses or a bandana for dust in the dry season.
* **Avoid riding back after dark:** Leave the lagoon by 4:30 PM since rural roads have no streetlights.
