## Overview

On Sabah's east coast near Sandakan, the **Lower Kinabatangan Wildlife Sanctuary** is one of the best places on Earth to spot Borneo's Big Five in the wild—**Bornean orangutans, pygmy elephants, proboscis monkeys, saltwater crocodiles, and rhinoceros hornbills**.

## Highlights

* **Sepilok Orangutan Rehabilitation Centre & Sun Bear Centre:** Walk elevated rainforest boardwalks to observe semi-wild orangutans and the world's smallest bears (Bornean sun bears).
* **Dawn & Dusk River Cruises (Sukau / Bilit):** Glide quietly in small boats along oxbow lakes and riverbanks when wildlife gathers in the trees.
* **Night Rainforest Walks:** Spot sleeping kingfishers, slow lorises, and glowing fungi with a local guide.

## Getting There

* **Flight:** 45-minute flight from Kota Kinabalu to **Sandakan (SDK)**, then 25 min to Sepilok and ~2h to Sukau.
* **Overland:** ~6 hours from Kota Kinabalu via Kundasang and Telupid.
