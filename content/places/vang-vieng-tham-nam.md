## Overview

Located 13 km north of town near Ban Phathao, **Tham Nam (Water Cave)** offers one of Vang Vieng's most unique adventures: floating on an inner tube by headlamp through a cool, spring-fed subterranean tunnel while pulling yourself along a guide rope past sparkling stalactites.

## Highlights

* **Underground Cave Tubing:** ~45 minutes inside the flooded cavern (headlamps and tubes provided on-site or with a local guide).
* **Tham Sang (Elephant Cave):** A 10-minute walk across rice paddies from Tham Nam—a small riverside Buddhist shrine containing a natural stalactite shaped like an elephant and a Buddha footprint.
* **Combine with Nam Song Kayaking:** Most half-day adventure combos pair **Tham Nam cave tubing** in the morning with a relaxed **kayak paddle** down the Nam Song River back to Vang Vieng.

## Seasonal Safety Note

* **Peak rainy season (Jul – Sep):** High water can flood the cave entrance completely—check conditions locally before riding north.
* **What to bring:** Dry bag for your phone, swimsuit, water shoes, and a towel.
