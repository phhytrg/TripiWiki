## Overview

Built in 1904 during the French colonial era for King Sisavang Vong, the **Royal Palace Museum (Haw Kham)** sits right in the heart of Luang Prabang at the foot of Mount Phousi. Right outside its gates, Sisavangvong Road transforms every evening into Laos's most atmospheric night market.

## Highlights

* **Haw Kham & Royal Hall:** See the glittering glass-mosaic throne room, royal gifts from world leaders, and vintage royal cars in the garage.
* **Haw Pha Bang:** The ornate golden pavilion in the palace courtyard housing the sacred *Phra Bang* Buddha statue that gave the city its name.
* **Night Market (5:00–10:00 PM):** Browse handwoven silk textiles, saa paper lanterns, and Hmong embroidery—then head to the **Food Alley** off the main street for *khao soi*, coconut pancakes (*khanom krok*), and grilled Mekong fish.

## Practical Tips

* **Palace hours:** Typically closes for lunch (11:30 AM–1:30 PM) and last entry is ~3:30 PM. Strict dress code and no photography inside the main palace building (lockers are provided).
