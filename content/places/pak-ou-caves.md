## Overview

Carved into a sheer limestone cliff 25 km upstream from Luang Prabang where the Nam Ou meets the Mekong, the **Pak Ou Caves** have been a sacred pilgrimage site for centuries. Locals leave damaged or retiring Buddha statues here, creating shelves packed with thousands of wooden figures.

## Two Caves to Explore

* **Tham Ting (Lower Cave):** Directly above the river jetty, naturally lit by the wide cave mouth with views out over the Mekong.
* **Tham Theung (Upper Cave):** A 10-minute climb up shaded stone steps; darker and quieter—bring your phone flashlight or rent a torch at the entrance.

## Getting There

* **Slow Boat (Recommended):** ~1.5–2 hours upstream from the Luang Prabang riverfront pier (1 hour back downstream). Most boats stop at **Ban Xang Hai** ("Whisky Village") along the way.
* **Overland + Ferry:** 45-minute drive/scooter north to Ban Pak Ou, then a 5-minute wooden ferry across the river.
