## Overview

To understand modern Laos, start at the **COPE Visitor Centre** (Cooperative Orthotic and Prosthetic Enterprise). Free to enter (donations welcome), it tells the story of the Secret War, unexploded ordnance (UXO), and the inspiring rehabilitation work helping survivors across the country.

## Suggested Afternoon–Evening Plan

1. **COPE Visitor Centre (2:00–4:00 PM):** Watch one of the short documentaries in the small theater and browse the exhibits on prosthetic design and rural outreach.
2. **Mekong Sunset at Chao Anouvong Park (5:30 PM):** Head to the riverside promenade as locals gather for aerobics, jogging, and sunset views across the river.
3. **Vientiane Night Market & Ban Anou (6:30 PM):** Stroll the red-tented riverfront market, then walk 5 minutes inland to **Ban Anou Night Market** for grilled skewers, papaya salad (*tam mak hoong*), and sticky rice.
