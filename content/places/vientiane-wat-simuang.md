## Overview

Built in 1563 over ancient Khmer ruins, **Wat Si Muang** is Vientiane's most spiritually active temple and home to the **Lak Muang** (City Pillar). Located right where **Thadeua Road** begins heading southeast along the Mekong toward Buddha Park, it is the traditional first stop before a journey.

## Highlights

* **Wrist-Blessing Ceremony (*Sai Sin*):** Monks tie a white cotton thread around your wrist while chanting a blessing for safe travel and good fortune (small donation appreciated).
* **Marigold & Incense Offerings:** Watch locals offer conical banana-leaf marigold trays (*mak beng*) at the gilded city pillar shrine.
* **Khmer Stupa Ruins:** Walk behind the main *sim* hall to see mossy laterite Angkor-era ruins hidden in the courtyard garden.

## Combine with Buddha Park

* **Bus #14 or Tuk-Tuk:** Wat Si Muang sits right on the main Thadeua Road corridor—visit in the morning, then continue southeast to **LDWDC Weaving Centre**, **Friendship Bridge**, and **Buddha Park**.
