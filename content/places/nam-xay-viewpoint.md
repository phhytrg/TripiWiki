## Overview

Once known only for river tubing, **Vang Vieng** is now one of Southeast Asia's best karst adventure hubs—offering steep viewpoint hikes, caving, kayaking on the Nam Song, and hot-air ballooning.

## Highlights

* **Nam Xay Viewpoint:** A 25–30 minute steep scramble up bamboo ladders to a summit with panoramic views of the valley. Go for sunrise to see mist rolling between the karsts.
* **Blue Lagoon 3 & 5:** Quieter alternatives further west than Blue Lagoon 1, surrounded by dramatic cliffs.
* **Pha Ngern (Silver Cliff):** Slightly longer hike with three viewpoints and far fewer crowds.

## Gear & Safety

* Wear proper grip shoes—the limestone mud gets very slippery after rain.
* Bring 1.5L of water and a headlamp if starting before dawn.
