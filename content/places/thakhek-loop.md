## Overview

The **Thakhek Loop** is Laos's answer to the Ha Giang Loop—450 km of winding roads through Khammouane Province, connecting limestone caves, turquoise springs, and remote villages.

## Suggested 4-Day Itinerary

1. **Day 1 — Thakhek to Thalang (~100 km):** Ride past Elephant Cave and Pha Katai viewpoint, crossing the Nakai Plateau's ghostly flooded forest.
2. **Day 2 — Thalang to Nahin / Kong Lor (~140 km):** Twist through the Phou Hin Poun limestone mountains and stop at Cool Springs (Khoun Kong Leng).
3. **Day 3 — Kong Lor Cave:** Take a wooden longtail boat 7.5 km straight through a pitch-black subterranean river cave inside the mountain.
4. **Day 4 — Kong Lor back to Thakhek (~180 km):** Return via Route 13 or detour to The Rock Viewpoint for ziplining over the karst forest.
