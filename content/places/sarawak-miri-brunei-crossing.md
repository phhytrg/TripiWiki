## Overview

Sitting just 35 km from the Brunei border, **Miri** is the launchpad for both **Gunung Mulu National Park** and **Niah National Park**, and the primary land gateway between **Sarawak (East Malaysia)** and **Brunei Darussalam**.

## How East Malaysia Connects to Brunei (Complete Route Guide)

Because **Brunei** is split into two parts nestled inside northern **Sarawak** (between Miri and Limbang/Lawas), overland travel between Sarawak and Sabah passes right through Brunei:

1. **Sarawak (Miri) → Brunei West (Sungai Tujoh Border):**
   * Drive or take a cross-border bus/taxi ~40 min north from Miri to **Sungai Tujoh (Malaysia/Brunei ICQ)**, then continue along the coastal highway through **Kuala Belait** and **Seria** to **Bandar Seri Begawan** (~2–2.5 hours total).
2. **Brunei → Temburong Bridge → Lawas → Sabah:**
   * Cross the spectacular **30 km Sultan Haji Omar Ali Saifuddien Bridge (Temburong Bridge)** across Brunei Bay—this bypasses the old Limbang detour!
   * Exit Brunei at **Labu (Temburong) → Mengkalap (Sarawak, Lawas)**, then cross the **Sindumin** checkpoint into **Sabah** toward Sipitang and Kota Kinabalu.
3. **Important Border Tips:**
   * **Passport pages:** An overland Miri → Brunei → Lawas → Sabah trip requires **multiple immigration stamps** (Malaysia exit, Brunei entry/exit, Sarawak entry, Sabah entry)—make sure your passport has at least 6 months validity and blank pages.
   * **Vehicle pass (VES):** If driving a Malaysian rental car or motorbike into Brunei, register online for Brunei's **Vehicle Entry System (VES)** pass before reaching the border.
   * **Skip the road:** Take the **Muara (Brunei) ↔ Labuan ↔ Kota Kinabalu** express ferry instead!

## Nearby in Miri

* **Niah Caves:** 1.5 hours south of Miri—massive prehistoric limestone caverns with 40,000-year-old archaeological history.
* **Lambir Hills National Park:** 30 minutes south—lush dipterocarp rainforest trails and waterfalls.
