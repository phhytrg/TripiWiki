## Overview

Tucked deep in northern Sarawak just south of Brunei's Temburong rainforest, **Gunung Mulu National Park** holds some of the largest cave chambers on Earth alongside dramatic karst spires and untouched primary jungle.

## Highlights

* **Deer Cave & Bat Exodus:** Walk a 3 km raised rainforest boardwalk to one of the world's largest cave passages, then watch **2–3 million wrinkle-lipped bats** spiral out into the dusk sky.
* **Wind Cave & Clearwater Cave:** Take a wooden longboat up the Melinau River to swim in the crystal-clear subterranean river emerging from Clearwater Cave.
* **The Pinnacles Trek (3D2N):** A challenging steep climb up Gunung Api to overlook 45-meter razor-sharp limestone blades rising out of the jungle canopy.

## Getting There (No Public Roads!)

* **Flight (Easiest):** 30-minute MASwings turboprop flight from **Miri (MYY)** (or 55 min from Kota Kinabalu / Kuching) straight into Mulu Airport (MZV), 5 minutes from Park HQ.
* **Brunei Connection:** Travelers in **Bandar Seri Begawan** typically cross the border to **Miri** (2h) to catch the short flight to Mulu.
