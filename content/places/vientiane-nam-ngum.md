## Overview

Spanning over 370 km² between Vientiane and Vang Vieng, **Nam Ngum Reservoir** (nicknamed "Sea of Laos") was formed when the Nam Ngum River was dammed in 1971, turning hundreds of hilltops into lush green islands. Just southeast lies **Phou Khao Khouay National Protected Area**, home to forest trails and **Tad Leuk Waterfall**.

## Highlights

* **Longtail Boat Island Cruise:** Hire a wooden boat at **Ban Thalad** pier to cruise past submerged-forest islets and tiny fishing villages.
* **Floating Seafood Lunch:** Eat freshly caught grilled Mekong/reservoir fish (*ping pa*), spicy papaya salad, and sticky rice on a covered bamboo boat or lakeside terrace.
* **Stop at Ban Keun Salt Village:** On the drive north from Vientiane, see traditional brine-evaporation salt pans still operated by local families.

## Getting There

* **Day Trip from Vientiane:** ~1.5–2 hours north via Route 10 or Route 13N by rental car, scooter, or hired minivan.
* **En Route to Vang Vieng:** Makes a scenic half-day detour if driving between Vientiane and Vang Vieng.
