## Overview

Located 7 km west of Vang Vieng across the Nam Song River, **Blue Lagoon 1** is the most famous of Vang Vieng's mineral-rich turquoise springs. Directly above the lagoon, a steep limestone staircase leads into **Tham Phu Kham (Golden Crab Cave)**—a cathedral-sized cavern lit by natural shafts of sunlight shining onto a reclining bronze Buddha.

## Local Tips

* **Go early:** Arrive before 9:30 AM to swim in glassy turquoise water before tour buses arrive.
* **Climb to the cave first:** Wear sturdy sandals or sneakers (not flip-flops) for the 10-minute steep rocky climb to Tham Phu Kham, then cool off in the lagoon afterward.
* **Want quieter lagoons?** Continue further west along the scenic dirt loop to **Blue Lagoon 3** (~17 km from town) for zip-lines, bamboo rafts, and far fewer crowds.

## Getting There

* **Scooter or Dune Buggy:** ~15–20 minutes west from central Vang Vieng (small toll at the bridge crossing).
* **Tuk-Tuk:** Easy half-day hire from town; combine with **Nam Xay Viewpoint** along the same road.
