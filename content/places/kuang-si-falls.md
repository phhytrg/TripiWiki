## Overview

**Kuang Si Falls** is famous for its milky-turquoise travertine pools. A shaded jungle boardwalk leads past the **Tat Kuang Si Bear Rescue Centre** up to the 60-meter main cascade.

## Local Tips

* **Beat the crowds:** Leave Luang Prabang around 7:30 AM. The park opens at 8:00 AM, and tour vans arrive after 10:30 AM.
* **Hike to the top:** Follow the steep trail on the left side of the main waterfall for a quieter viewpoint and natural infinity pool at the top.
* **Respectful dress:** Cover shoulders and knees when walking outside the designated swimming pools.

## Transport

* **Shared Minivan:** ~40 minutes from Luang Prabang Old Town.
* **Scooter:** Scenic 30 km ride along paved rural roads passing Hmong and Khmu villages.
