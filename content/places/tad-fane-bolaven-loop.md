## Overview

Rising 1,000–1,350 meters above the Mekong valley, the **Bolaven Plateau** is Laos's coffee heartland and one of the best multi-day motorbike loops in southern Laos.

## Highlights

* **Tad Fane:** Twin 120-meter waterfalls plunging into a deep jungle gorge; famous for ziplining across the canyon.
* **Tad Yuang & Tad Champee:** Easy-access waterfalls nearby where you can swim in the dry season.
* **Coffee Plantations:** Stop at family-run Arabica and Robusta roasteries around Paksong and Lak 40 (e.g., Mr. Vieng or Jhai Coffee).
* **Combine with Si Phan Don (4000 Islands):** Head south from Pakse to Don Det & Don Khon to see Mekong river dolphins and Khone Phapheng Falls.
