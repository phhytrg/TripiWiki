## Overview

Located about 15 km southeast of Luang Prabang, **Tad Sae Waterfall** flows over broad, stepped travertine shelves into shallow, swimmable turquoise pools shaded by tropical forest. Because it is fed by seasonal streams, it is at its most impressive during and right after the rainy season.

## Seasonal Note

* **Best months (Aug – Dec):** Lush, full turquoise cascades and clear swimming pools.
* **Late dry season (Feb – May):** Water levels can drop significantly or dry up—check locally in Luang Prabang before going, or head to Kuang Si Falls instead.

## Getting There & Nearby Stops

* **Boat crossing:** Ride a tuk-tuk or scooter (~25 min) to **Ban En** pier on the Nam Khan River, then take a 10-minute wooden longtail boat straight to the waterfall entrance.
* **Ban Phanom Weaving Village:** Stop on the way back to see traditional Lue handloom silk and cotton weaving.
