## Overview

**Kota Kinabalu (KK)** is the vibrant gateway to Malaysian Borneo—famous for its fiery sunsets over the South China Sea, fresh night-market seafood, and coral islands just 15 minutes off Jesselton Point Pier.

## Highlights

* **Tunku Abdul Rahman Marine Park:** Hop by speedboat from Jesselton Point to **Manukan**, **Sapi**, and **Mamutik** islands for snorkeling and beach trails.
* **Tanjung Aru Beach & Filipino Market:** Watch sunset over the sea, then feast on grilled fish, *hinava*, and Sabah seafood along the waterfront.
* **Sabah State Railway (Beaufort → Tenom):** Ride Borneo's historic metre-gauge jungle train through the Padas River Gorge south of KK.

## Connecting Kota Kinabalu ↔ Brunei (2 Best Ways)

1. **Fast Ferry via Labuan Island (Simplest — Fewer Border Stamps):**
   * Take the morning express ferry from **Jesselton Point (KK) → Labuan** (~3h 30m), then connect to the **Labuan → Serasa Ferry Terminal (Muara, Brunei)** (~1h 15m).
   * From Muara, it's a 30-minute Dart ride/taxi into **Bandar Seri Begawan**.
2. **Overland Bus / Drive (Sipitang → Lawas → Brunei):**
   * Take the **Sipitang Express** bus or drive south along the Pan Borneo Highway: **KK → Beaufort → Sipitang → Sindumin (Sabah/Sarawak border) → Lawas → Labu / Temburong Bridge → Bandar Seri Begawan** (~6–7 hours, passport required for multiple Brunei/Malaysia checkpoints).
