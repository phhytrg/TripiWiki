## Overview

Set at the confluence of the Mekong and Nam Khan rivers, **Luang Prabang** blends traditional Lao timber houses, French-Indochina shophouses, and over 30 active Buddhist monasteries.

## Highlights

* **Tak Bat (Morning Alms):** Observe silently from a respectful distance around 5:30–6:00 AM on Sakkaline Road. Avoid flash photography.
* **Wat Xieng Thong:** The 16th-century royal temple at the tip of the peninsula with sweeping multi-tiered roofs.
* **Mount Phousi:** Climb the 328 steps for 360-degree sunset views over the Mekong, or go at sunrise for a peaceful climb.
* **Nam Khan Bamboo Bridge:** Rebuilt every dry season; leads to quiet weaving villages on the opposite bank.
