## Overview

Rising 4,095 meters above northern Borneo, **Mount Kinabalu** is the centerpiece of Sabah and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Even if you don't summit, the surrounding **Kundasang** highlands (1,500–1,900 m) offer cool mountain air, botanical trails, and dramatic dawn views of the jagged granite massif.

## Highlights

* **2-Day Summit Climb:** Trek 6 km from Timpohon Gate to Panalaban (3,272 m) on Day 1, then push for **Low's Peak (4,095 m)** at 2:30 AM for sunrise above the clouds. Permits & mountain guides must be booked months ahead.
* **Desa Dairy Farm & Sosodikon Hill:** Iconic sunrise viewpoints in Kundasang facing the mountain ridge.
* **Poring Hot Springs & Canopy Walk:** 40 minutes east of Kinabalu Park—soak tired legs in sulfur pools and walk 41 meters above the rainforest floor.

## Getting There

* **From Kota Kinabalu:** 2 hours by minivan or rental car along the winding Crocker Range highway.
