## Overview

Built in 1958 by mystic sculptor Bunleua Sulilat, **Xieng Khuan (Buddha Park)** sits right along the Mekong River past the Thai–Laos Friendship Bridge. Its weathered concrete sculptures look centuries old and blend Buddhist and Hindu mythology.

## Don't Miss

* **40-Meter Reclining Buddha:** The park's centerpiece, stretching across the southern edge of the lawn.
* **The Giant Pumpkin Tower:** Enter through a demon's mouth and climb three narrow stories representing Hell, Earth, and Heaven to a rooftop panorama over the whole park.
* **Riverside Tea Stop:** Grab a cold Lao iced coffee or coconut at the shaded riverside cafe overlooking Thailand.

## Getting There

* **Public Bus #14:** Departs from Vientiane's Central Bus Station (CBS) near Talat Sao every ~20 minutes (~45 min ride, very cheap).
* **LOCA Taxi / Tuk-Tuk:** ~35 minutes from central Vientiane; easy to combine with a stop near the Friendship Bridge.
