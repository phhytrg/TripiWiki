Laos–China Railway (LCR) & Cross-Border Trains

The fastest, most comfortable way to travel between Vientiane, Vang Vieng, Luang Prabang, and the Chinese border—plus the overnight rail link to Bangkok via Khamsavath.

Interactive Railway & Station Map

1. Laos–China Railway (LCR)

Operating at up to 160 km/h, the LCR cuts overland travel times in northern Laos from 8+ hours on mountain buses down to 1–2 hours.

2. Vientiane ↔ Bangkok (Khamsavath Station)

For travelers arriving from or heading to Thailand, the Thai–Laos metre-gauge railway connects Vientiane (Khamsavath Station) across the Mekong Friendship Bridge to Nong Khai and overnight sleeper trains to Bangkok (Krung Thep Aphiwat).

3. Station Transfers & Practical Tips

  • Stations are outside town: Vientiane LCR (14 km), Luang Prabang (12 km), and Vang Vieng (4 km) all require a 10–30 minute tuk-tuk, shared minivan, or LOCA taxi.
  • Booking tickets: Use the official LCR Ticket app (opens 3 days in advance, requires passport details), buy at the station, or book through local guesthouses / 12Go.
  • Airport-style security: Arrive 45–60 minutes early. No aerosols/sprays, pocket knives, or large power banks (>20,000 mAh) in carry-on bags.

Key Stations

  • Vientiane LCR — Capital hub
  • Khamsavath — Bangkok sleeper link
  • Vang Vieng — Karst & lagoons
  • Luang Prabang — UNESCO Old Town
  • Muang Xay — Bus link to Nong Khiaw
  • Nateuy — Bus link to Luang Namtha
  • Boten — China border / Kunming
