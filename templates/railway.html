🇮🇩 Indonesia — Whoosh High-Speed Railway (350 km/h)

Southeast Asia's first true bullet train (KCIC Whoosh) links Jakarta and Bandung in 30–45 minutes at speeds up to 350 km/h, slashing a 3–4 hour highway drive.

Route Duration Top Speed Transfer Tip
Jakarta (Halim) ↔ Karawang ~15 min 350 km/h Direct LRT Jabodebek link at Halim
Jakarta (Halim) ↔ Padalarang ~30 min 350 km/h Free 18-min KA Feeder train into central Bandung
Jakarta (Halim) ↔ Tegalluar ~45 min 350 km/h Shuttle buses to Summarecon & East Bandung

Booking: Official Whoosh app, Access by KAI, or station kiosks.

🇱🇦 Laos — Laos–China Railway (LCR, 160 km/h)

Operating modern standard-gauge EMU trains up to 160 km/h, the LCR connects Vientiane, Vang Vieng, Luang Prabang, and the Chinese border (Boten/Kunming), with cross-border metre-gauge trains at Khamsavath linking directly to Bangkok.

Route EMU Express Top Speed Notes
Vientiane ↔ Vang Vieng ~50 min – 1 hr 160 km/h Ordinary train: ~1h 20m (120 km/h)
Vang Vieng ↔ Luang Prabang ~50 min 160 km/h Mountain tunnels & Mekong bridges
Vientiane ↔ Luang Prabang ~1 hr 50 min – 2 hr 160 km/h Book 2–3 days ahead in high season
Vientiane ↔ Boten (China Border) ~3 hr 30 min 160 km/h International D887/D888 continues to Kunming

Booking & Security: LCR Ticket app (opens 3 days ahead); arrive 45–60 min early for airport-style security.

🇲🇾 Malaysia — KTM ETS (160 km/h) & Sabah State Railway

Peninsular Malaysia runs the fast, electrified KTM ETS (140–160 km/h) from the Thai border at Padang Besar through Penang and Ipoh to KL Sentral (plus the 160 km/h KLIA Ekspres airport train). In East Malaysia (Borneo), the scenic Sabah State Railway winds from Kota Kinabalu through the Padas River Gorge to Tenom.

Route Duration Top Speed Service & Notes
KL Sentral ↔ Ipoh ~2 hr 10 min 140–160 km/h KTM ETS Platinum / Gold
KL Sentral ↔ Butterworth (Penang) ~4 hr 140–160 km/h KTM ETS · 5-min walk to Penang ferry
KL Sentral ↔ Padang Besar (Thai Border) ~5 hr 15 min 140–160 km/h KTM ETS · Direct transfer to Thailand
Tanjung Aru (KK) ↔ Beaufort ↔ Tenom ~3 hr 30 min 60–80 km/h Sabah State Railway · Padas Gorge

Booking: Official KTMB Mobile (KITS) app for ETS; buy tickets at Tanjung Aru / Beaufort counters for Sabah State Railway.

🇹🇭 Thailand — SRT Intercity & Thai–Lao HSR Corridor

Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal (Bangkok) is Southeast Asia's central rail hub—linking north to Nong Khai / Vientiane (Laos) (where Thailand's 250 km/h Bangkok–Korat–Nong Khai High-Speed Railway is under construction) and south to Hat Yai / Padang Besar (Malaysia).

Route Duration Top Speed Cross-Border Link
Bangkok ↔ Nakhon Ratchasima (Korat) ~4 hr (HSR: ~1 hr 15 min) 100 km/h (HSR: 250 km/h) Phase 1 Thai–Chinese HSR corridor
Bangkok ↔ Nong Khai ↔ Vientiane ~10–11 hr overnight 90–120 km/h Direct train to Vientiane Khamsavath (Laos)
Bangkok ↔ Hua Hin ~3 hr 30 min 100–120 km/h Upgraded double-track Southern Line
Bangkok ↔ Padang Besar (Malaysia) ~15–16 hr overnight 90–120 km/h Connects directly to Malaysia's KTM ETS to KL

Booking: Official SRT D-Ticket portal (`dticket.railway.co.th`) up to 180 days in advance.
